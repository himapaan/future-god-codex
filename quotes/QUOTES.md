# Selected excerpts

60 excerpts from *Future God*, 3,420 words in total, selected to
represent approximately three-quarters of the principal conceptual ground in the
Introduction, every chapter, and the Afterword. The selection is deliberately a
companion to the book, not a substitute for reading it.

Every blockquote is reproduced verbatim from the print edition (v1.1, first
printing) and checked mechanically against that PDF. Concept labels and context
notes are editorial metadata: they describe the passages but are not themselves
quotations from the book. The verification result is recorded in
[VERIFICATION.md](VERIFICATION.md).

[`quotes.json`](quotes.json) is the source of truth for this page. If the two
ever disagree, the JSON is correct, and `tools/check_repo.py` fails until they
match again.

Each excerpt lies wholly within one printed page, none begins at a chapter’s
drop-cap opener, and none reproduces language the book itself quotes from
someone else. Licensing and scope: [../NOTICE.md](../NOTICE.md).

The section-level coverage figures are an editorial map of principal concepts,
not a quantitative claim about how much of the book’s prose is reproduced. The
excerpts total 14.75% of the reviewed source body.
